# Live demo: exact flow (about 5 minutes)

**Before you start:** Admin tab → **Reset demo**. The Farmer tab shows two phones: a **button phone** (left) and a **smartphone** (right). The Expert tab is on the sign-in list. The dealer phone is logged in (PIN 1111) with alerts turned on.

Keyboard works on the Farmer tab: number keys, **Enter** = green call key, **Esc** = red end key.

## 1. A button-phone farmer gets an AI answer (Farmer tab, 1 min)
1. Press the **green call key** on the button phone. Its screen shows only "In call" and a timer, like a real one. The **On the line** panel captions what the farmer hears.
2. Press **1** (Punjabi), **1** (skip the notice), **1** (tehsil correct), **1** (wheat), **1** (under 3 days).
3. After the **beep** the phone is listening. Say slowly:
   **"Meri gandum de pattiyan te sittiyan te nikke nikke sabz keere lagge ne. Tela lag gaya ae. Ki karaan?"**
   Then press **any key**. (Backup: the **Common** button under the phone.)
4. The AI answers by voice. Point at **How the AI decided**: the source entry, the farmer's own words found in it, and "word for word".

> Say: "This is the phone most of our farmers own. No app, no internet, no reading. The AI does not write advice. It reads an answer agronomists wrote, and shows exactly which one and why."

## 2. A hard question goes to a human (Farmer tab, 40 s)
1. Press any key, then **1** (new question). After the beep press the **Hard** button (sugarcane red rot).
2. The helpline says an expert will call back. The panel shows "Not covered by knowledge base".
3. Press any key, **2** (end call), then **2** (delete the recording).

> Say: "It is not in our knowledge base, so the AI does not guess. And the recording is kept only if the farmer says yes."

## 3. Junior drafts, senior approves (Expert tab, 1 min 20 s)
1. Tap **Hamza Tariq** (junior). Home shows what is waiting; tap **Open next question** or go to **Questions** and open the sugarcane one.
2. Paste this and tap **Send draft for review**:
   یہ کماد کا رتا روگ لگتا ہے۔ متاثرہ گنے جڑ سمیت نکال کر کھیت سے باہر تلف کریں۔ متاثرہ کھیت کا پانی دوسرے کھیتوں میں نہ جانے دیں۔ اگلی بار صحت مند بیج لگائیں۔
3. **Back** → **Profile** → **Switch account** → **Dr. Ayesha Malik** (senior).
4. **Questions** → the one marked **Draft to review**. After **رتا روگ** add **(ریڈ راٹ)**. The diff shows your change.
5. Tap **Approve with edits**.

> Say: "Juniors are fresh graduates who can only draft. A senior approves, and the junior's score depends on how much the senior had to change."

## 4. The button phone rings (Farmer tab, 30 s)
1. The button phone shakes and shows "Incoming call". Press the **green call key**. The approved answer plays.

> Say: "The farmer gets a normal call back, on the same phone."

## 5. The AI learns (Expert tab, 20 s)
1. Tap **Add to knowledge base**, then open the **Knowledge** tab: the new entry is at the top, marked "From an expert answer".

> Say: "Now this answer is knowledge. The next farmer with this problem gets it instantly."

## 6. The smartphone door (Farmer tab, 40 s, optional)
1. On the smartphone, tap the **camera**, pick a leaf photo, choose the crop, and tap **send**. The reply arrives in the chat with a voice note.
2. Optional: on the Expert tab tap **Video call the farmer's smartphone** and accept on the smartphone.

> Say: "Farmers with a smartphone in the family can send a photo or take a video call. Same safety rules behind it."

## 7. Outbreak alert (Admin tab and dealer phone, 1 min)
1. Admin tab: tap **Trigger outbreak**. The Okara counter goes 1, 2, 3.
2. Hold up the **dealer phone**: it buzzes with "Outbreak near you: Okara". Tick the stock checklist and tap **Confirm stock is ready**.

> Say: "Three farmers in Okara reported yellow rust this week. Every farmer there gets a voice alert, and dealers know what to stock before demand hits."

## 8. Proof it is safe (Admin tab, 20 s)
1. Tap **Run evaluation**.

> Say: "56 test questions in Urdu, Punjabi and Roman Urdu. Zero unsafe answers. When it is unsure, it sends the question to a human."

## 9. Optional: 3-day follow-up (Admin and Farmer tabs, 30 s)
1. Admin tab: **3-day follow-up calls**. The button phone rings; answer, then press **2** (still a problem). The question goes back to the experts.

## Close
> "AI where it's safe, humans where it matters, and a network that sees outbreaks first."

**If anything stalls:** use the sample buttons under the button phone, or Admin → **Reset demo** and continue from the current step.
